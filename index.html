Getting there

Venue address:
Minstead Lodge, Seaman’s Lane, Minstead, SO43 7FT

By train + taxi
If you’re coming from London, Devon, Liverpool or elsewhere in the UK, the nearest train station is Brockenhurst, about 1.5 hrs from London Waterloo and a short taxi ride to Lyndhurst or Minstead.

By car
Minstead Lodge has ample on-site parking. Lyndhurst and the Lodge are accessible via the A337 from Cadnam, which links into the M27 (with onward motorway connections).

If you prefer to drive to your accommodation in Lyndhurst, or drive directly to the venue (and leave your car there overnight), we will provide a coach service to and from the venue so no one has to drive after the celebrations.

One thing to watch for: free-roaming ponies, deer, and other forest animals are common here — slow down, especially on forest roads.

Coach & taxi service on the day
We’ll be running coaches from a few hotels in Lyndhurst (see more in the Accommodation section). Pick-ups will take place late morning so we can arrive at the venue by 1pm, and return coaches will be available at the end of the night to drop you back at your hotels (more information to follow closer to the day).

If you’d rather not use the coach service, local taxis are an option — but we recommend booking in advance if you can.

Accommodation

We’ll be based in Lyndhurst for the weekend and are staying at the Crown Hotel, where we’ve block-booked a number of rooms for our guests. If you’d like to stay there too, call and let them know you’re part of Kate & Neil's wedding group.

We’ve also set aside rooms at the [Hotel Name 1], and [Hotel Name 2] at agreed rates; they’ll have the same booking reference ("Kate & Neil") if you prefer these options.

We’ll be running coaches from these three hotels on the day, picking guests up late morning (for arrival at the venue by 1pm) and taking everyone back to Lyndhurst at the end of the night around 11:30pm.

Local taxi services are available too, though it’s worth booking these in advance.

There are plenty of lovely places to stay in and around Lyndhurst, so if none of these feel quite right, feel free to choose somewhere that suits you best.
